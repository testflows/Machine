# Code of Conduct

This repository is a professional space for TestFlows Machine users to report
problems, share ideas and help each other. This code applies to issues,
pull requests, discussions and every other place in it.

## Expected behavior

* Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
* Stay on topic, and give others the information they need to help you.
* Assume good intent, and help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behavior

* Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
* Offensive or discriminatory language or content.
* Spam, advertising or deliberately off-topic posts.
* Publishing someone else's private information, including credentials,
  without their permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to `abuse@testflows.com`. We review every report
and keep the reporter's identity confidential.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or delete content that breaks this code, and
may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who breaks it.
